Skandha Living

Design that gathers everything a home needs into one whole.

The word skandha means a bundle, a gathering of parts into a whole. That is how we approach a home: light, layout, materials, furniture and the small things you touch every day, all designed together instead of one room at a time.

We work from Bengaluru and design for the city's climate, its light and the way families here actually live.

Our philosophy

Three ideas behind every room

Every Skandha Living project starts with the same three questions, whatever the size of the home.

Rooted

Materials and motifs drawn from where we live: teak, stone, cane, brass and handloom, chosen to age well in Bengaluru's weather.

Tailored

No two families are alike. Layouts, storage and finishes are planned around your routines, your guests and your belongings.

Enduring

We design for the long run: sound joinery, honest finishes and layered palettes that outlast trends.

How we work

A clear path from first call to move-in

Discover

We visit the site, listen to how you live and agree on scope, budget and timeline.

Design

Mood boards, layouts and 3D views so you can see your home before anything is built.

Build

We coordinate carpenters, electricians and vendors, and check quality on site each week.

Style and hand over

Furniture, lighting and finishing touches are installed so you can walk into a finished home.

The studio

A Bengaluru studio for layered, livable rooms

Skandha Living is a residential design studio led by a small team of designers, architects and project managers. We take on a limited number of homes at a time so each one gets close attention.

Our rooms are warm, layered and comfortable, built with good proportions, natural materials and enough personality to feel like you.
