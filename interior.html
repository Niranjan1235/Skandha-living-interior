Dmar Interiors is a full-service residential design studio based in Southern California. With hundreds of projects nationwide and over 20 years of experience, our award-winning work has been featured on HGTV and in top-tier publications worldwide.

​Our Philosophy

California Modern Interiors through our 3-T Method

​Tasteful

We believe in thoughtful restraint - layered palettes, subtle pattern plays, and materials chosen for both beauty and longevity.

Tailored 

No two homes, or homeowners, are alike. Our space reflects the individuality of the people who live in them. 

Timeless

Grounded in architectural integrity, our work avoids fleeting trends in favor of enduring design.
