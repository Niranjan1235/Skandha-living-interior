ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Spaces Built Around Your Vision

Skandha Groups is a Bengaluru-based company working across residential construction, interior design and property sales. We keep every project grounded in how people actually live: the light in a room, the flow of a floor plan, the finish on a wall.

Whether you are building a home, furnishing one, or looking for a flat to buy, you deal with one team that understands the whole journey.
